# Phase 1.5 semantic-resolution evaluation contract

`evals/phase1_5_semantic_resolution.yaml` is frozen at 15 real corpus terms. It defines unambiguous canonical mappings, acceptable aliases, explicitly ambiguous cases, and confusable concepts. Phase 2 must report Top-1 accuracy and Top-k recall only for unambiguous cases, preserve ambiguity where marked, and demonstrate separation of airflow velocity from flame-spread velocity and extinction from suppression. No embedding was generated in Phase 1.5.
