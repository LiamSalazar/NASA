# Grounding policy

A future LLM may interpret wording to known concepts and organize/summarize `ScientificAnswer` evidence. It may explain already-computed differences and quote/paraphrase retrieved NASA evidence. It may not create facts, causality, equivalence, guidance, open questions, broaden applicability, hide no-direct status, or use model knowledge to replace sources. Scientists retain inference, hypothesis, design, applicability, and new-knowledge decisions. Future adapters receive only `ScientificAnswer`, never raw-corpus unrestricted access or graph mutation authority.
