# Structured NASA combustion holdout search

The search was bounded to the official NASA PSI research-area/investigation
catalog and existing NASA PSI/NTRS acquisition metadata. NASA's current
[PSI investigations by research area](https://www.nasa.gov/physical-sciences-informatics-psi/psi-investigations-by-research-area/)
list includes the combustion programs screened by the repository's prior
candidate history (including BASS, FLEX, SAFFIRE, SAME, SLICE, and SPICE).
The
previous candidate pools, and the post-repair frozen architecture. The complete
reproducible structured candidate subset is retained in
`artifacts/phase3c_final_repair_structured_candidates_v2.json`, with source pool
digest, ordering, prior exposure/preflight, structural metadata, and exclusion
state. The final-repair freeze digest is attached to that artifact.

No new eligible, unexposed structured NASA combustion experimental dataset was
acquired. The remaining PSI candidates in the prior pool had already been
structurally exposed during earlier Phase 3C screening or lacked a usable
experimental table; candidate sources whose data had already entered canonical
or evaluation/semantic-design state were excluded. Previously exposed PSI-142,
PSI-99, FLEX and other earlier sources were not reused as new holdouts. The
selected source is null; no source was replaced after an unfavorable result.

Consequently there is no new blind first-pass mapping, row-classification, or
native retrieval score to report in this iteration. Earlier structured and
documentary holdout receipts remain preserved and are not relabeled as new.
This is a bounded search outcome, not a claim that NASA has no qualifying
dataset. Generalization remains LIMITED until a genuinely unexposed structured
NASA source is selected, frozen, acquired, and evaluated.
